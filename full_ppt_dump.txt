=== SLIDE 11 ===
[Group Group 2 id=2 with 2 children]:
[Group Group 5 id=5 with 2 children]:
  [Text TextBox 6 id=6]: "Existing crop-yield prediction approaches frequently represent precipitation using contemporaneous or aggregated features. This methodology potentially obscures delayed and crop-specific rainfall responses. The contributions of rainfall at various time lags—such as t-1, t-2, t-4, and t-8—remain largely unknown. This lack of representation can lead to inaccurate yield forecasts, as it does not account for the complex physiological and agronomic responses of crops to delayed precipitation. A more nuanced approach that incorporates lagged rainfall data is crucial for improving the accuracy of yield predictions in Kerala's agricultural landscape."
  [Text TextBox 7 id=7]: 'Understanding the limitations of existing models'
